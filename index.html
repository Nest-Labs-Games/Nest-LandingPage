About

A game accelerator that provides mentorship, tools, market data, and seed funding for gamedevs

Indonesia has the talent, but three structural gaps keep hit games from shipping mobile hits: no end to end mobile dev education (especially in Game Direction and Business), no capital, and a lingering premium first mindset. Nest closes all three, investing in early stage Indonesian studios and taking them from prototype to market, milestone by milestone, across Game Direction, production, monetization, and Live Ops.

What to Prepare

Before you apply

Simply submit your portfolio to get started. No upfront prototype, fees, or contracts required. You'll create your game later, during the program.

Who can apply

  • Indonesian studios or individual developers (18+) with an existing game portfolio.
  • Ready to fully commit to the 6 week bootcamp.
How to Apply

Submit your portfolio, team profile, or mobile pitch deck via our online form.

Engine

Games must be built in Unity.

Genres

Developers of any platform or genre can apply — but the game you build in the program must be mobile: Casual, Puzzle, Idle/Incremental, Simulation, Arcade, and more.

Team Size

No minimum or maximum. Solo developers are welcome.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a finished, shipped game to apply?+

No. A polished concept or a vertical slice is enough for your portfolio at this stage.

What happens if I'm not selected for Stage 03, Developer in Residence?+

You can still redeem your completion grant, as long as you finish all mentorship sessions and graduate the cohort.

Is there a fee to apply?+

No, there's no fee at any stage.

I'm a solo developer. What does "matched into a team" mean?+

We believe plenty of solo developers can deliver hit games on their own. Keep building solo, or connect with other developers in our community later if you want to team up.

Who owns the IP of my game?+

Your studio keeps 100% of the IP.

Can non-Indonesian developers apply?+

For Batch 1, the program is open to Indonesian developers and studios only.

Can I apply again if I don't make it into Batch 1?+

Yes, as long as you've grown and learned from the feedback since your last attempt.
