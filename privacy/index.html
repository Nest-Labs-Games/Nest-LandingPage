Privacy Policy

For mobile games published by Nest Labs Games
Effective date: 23 November 2026

In short: our games don't ask for your name, email or contacts. Like most free mobile games, they collect device and gameplay data so the game works, so we can fix crashes and understand how the game is played, and so ads can be shown. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings and ask us to delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to every mobile game published under the developer account Nest Labs Games on Google Play ("we", "us"). Nest Labs runs the Nest Game Incubator and publishes games developed by partner studios. In this policy, "the games" means these games, including:

  • Sand Claw
  • Grass Collect
  • Cat's Chase
  • Courier Merge: Tap Delivery
  • Meow Match
  • Big Fish
  • Squish-In
  • Ninja Smash

and any other game whose Google Play listing links to this page. We are responsible for the personal data processed through the games.

2. What data we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or give us your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The games and the services built into them may collect:

3. Third-party services

The games use third-party software (SDKs) that may collect the data above directly from your device, under their own privacy policies. Depending on the game, these can include:

  • Google AdMob and Google Ad Manager (ads): Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from partner apps
  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics (analytics and crash reports): Firebase privacy
  • Unity (game engine, Unity Analytics and Unity Ads): Unity Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin (ads and mediation): AppLovin Privacy Policy
  • Other ad networks used through mediation, each listed in the game's consent screen where one is required.

We do not sell your personal data. Advertising partners may use your advertising ID to show ads that are more relevant to you; see section 5 to turn this off.

4. Legal basis

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies, we process data because it is needed to provide the game you chose to play (contract), for our legitimate interest in keeping the games working, secure and improving, and, for personalised advertising, on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the games ask for consent before personalised ads are shown.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Personalised ads: on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalised.
  • Consent: where a consent screen is shown, you can change your choice from the game's settings menu.
  • Access, correction and deletion: you can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us (section 10). Tell us the game name; we may ask for your device's installation ID to find your data.
  • Uninstalling the game stops all further collection.

Depending on where you live (for example under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP No. 27/2022), the EU/UK GDPR, or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Children

The games are general-audience games and are not directed primarily at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Where a game may be played by children, we limit data collection and show only non-personalised, age-appropriate ads in line with the Google Play Families Policy. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. How long we keep data

We keep analytics and crash data only as long as it is useful for running and improving the games, normally no longer than 14 months, and then delete or anonymise it. Third-party services keep data under their own retention policies.

8. Security and international transfers

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our service providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States and Singapore. Where required, these transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our games or the law change. The effective date at the top will always show the latest version. For significant changes, we will give notice in the game or on the Google Play listing.
